Terms & conditions

Read these project-use terms before entering the research workspace.

01 / Educational purpose

Credit Ledger is a portfolio and educational demonstration. It is not a bank service, credit bureau, lending decision system or financial advisory service. Do not use its outputs to approve, reject, price or service actual loans.

02 / Data and calculations

All eight obligors and their financial inputs are synthetic. Expected loss uses a simplified 12-month PD × LGD × EAD calculation. Stage labels are illustrative and the application does not calculate IFRS 9 expected credit losses.

03 / Explanations and limitations

Attribution bars are illustrative additive contributions, not computed SHAP values. Scenario outputs are arithmetic sensitivities, not causal predictions. No trained model, statistical confidence interval, validated performance result or measured drift is provided. Independently verify calculations and assumptions before extending this project.

04 / Privacy and local processing

This version has no account system, analytics, external fonts or application backend. Search and scenario inputs are processed in your browser. CSV exports are generated locally. The application stores no acceptance record. A hosting provider may separately process request information under its own policies.

05 / Responsible use

Do not present these fixtures as real customer records or these results as validated credit decisions. Do not add personal borrower data, credentials or confidential documents to a public repository. You are responsible for reviewing any data and model you later connect.
