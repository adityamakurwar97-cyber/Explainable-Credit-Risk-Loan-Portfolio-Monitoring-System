Methodology & interpretation

01. Twelve-month expected loss (EL) = EAD × 12m PD × LGD. Calculations use full precision; displayed amounts are rounded. Portfolio PD is weighted by EAD. Totals always cover all eight obligors; search and filters affect the register and CSV only.

02. Stage labels are illustrative review assignments. This dashboard does not calculate IFRS 9 ECL: Stage 2 requires lifetime risk, discounting and forward-looking scenarios; Stage 3 requires credit-impaired cash-flow analysis. PD alone does not assign a stage.

03. Signed contributions are synthetic, additive percentage-point explanations, constructed to reconcile baseline PD to the displayed PD. They are SHAP-style graphics, not computed SHAP values or causal effects. Scenario adjustments are arithmetic sensitivities, not model predictions.

Known limitation

The original UCI credit-card schema cannot support commercial-loan DSCR, collateral or covenant analysis. These fixtures replace that demo; they do not reinterpret credit limits as EAD.

No statistical confidence interval is available. The drill-down shows a labeled sensitivity range instead. Neither fixture values nor that range establish model accuracy.

Analyst note: a precise number is not a validated estimate.
